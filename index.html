The story behind AVENZAQ

Built from an opportunity to learn, grow and prepare for the future.

ZAKARIYAH ADAM OLAMILEKAN

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Before starting his journey into higher education, Zakariyah Adam Olamilekan was preparing to pursue Computer Science and had a period of time before resuming school. Rather than allow that time to pass without building something valuable, he decided to explore an industry he believed was becoming increasingly important: solar energy.

What began as an opportunity to learn solar installation developed into a much bigger vision. Zakariyah wanted to understand the industry beyond installation — from system design and maintenance to solar equipment, energy storage and the growing range of products being developed around modern power solutions.

He believes the world is moving toward smarter, cleaner and more reliable ways of producing and using energy. That belief became one of the foundations for AVENZAQ.

Today, the vision goes beyond installing solar systems. The goal is to build AVENZAQ into a complete energy and power solutions company — supplying solar equipment and accessories, delivering professional installations and maintenance, and eventually providing modern energy products such as solar generators, power stations, solar refrigerators and other emerging solutions.

The ambition is simple: start small, keep learning, build properly and grow AVENZAQ into a company capable of serving customers across Nigeria and, eventually, beyond.

Questions

Solar and power solutions, explained simply.

Not sure what system or equipment you need? Here are answers to common questions. You can also contact AVENZAQ for guidance.

Can AVENZAQ help me choose the right solar system?

Yes. Tell us what you want to power, where you are located and how you want to use the system. We can help assess the power requirement and recommend a suitable configuration.

Do I need to know my exact wattage before requesting a quote?

No. You can simply list your appliances or equipment and your typical usage. We can help with the technical assessment and system sizing.

Does AVENZAQ supply solar equipment?

Yes. AVENZAQ offers solar equipment and accessories including panels, inverters, batteries, charge controllers, cables, protection equipment and other power products, subject to current availability.

Do you provide solar maintenance and repairs?

Yes. AVENZAQ provides maintenance, troubleshooting, repairs and system upgrades for solar and backup-power systems.

Where does AVENZAQ operate?

AVENZAQ is based in Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria, with a long-term goal of serving customers across Nigeria and eventually beyond.
